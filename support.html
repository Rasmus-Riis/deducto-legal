Deducto Support

Deducto turns photos of receipts into a tax-ready expense spreadsheet. Snap a receipt, and the vendor, date, amount, and tax category are extracted automatically.

Do I need an account?

No. Deducto starts working straight away — no sign-up, no email required. Your first 4 scans are free. Creating an account (Settings → Create a free account) is optional and only needed if you want to reach your receipts from another device. After the free scans, Deducto Pro unlocks unlimited scanning and Excel export for $39.99/year.

Need help? Email support@deducto.pro and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

How do I fix a receipt the app read incorrectly?

Tap the receipt in your Receipts list. Every field — vendor, date, amount, and tax category — is editable. Saving marks the receipt as verified.

How do I export my receipts?

On the Receipts tab, tap Export. Deducto builds an Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheet of all your receipts and opens the share sheet so you can send it to your accountant, save it to Files, or AirDrop it.

How do I change my tax categories?

Settings → Manage tax categories. You can rename, remove, and add categories — new scans are sorted into your custom set automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Deducto Pro → Cancel. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

What happens to my receipt photos?

Your original receipt photo is stored securely in your private account and is only used to read and display your receipt. It is deleted automatically when you delete the receipt, or when you delete your account.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account & all data. This permanently removes your account, every receipt photo, and all extracted data from our servers. It cannot be undone.

Which countries are supported?

Deducto currently supports US tax categories (Schedule C style). More countries are on the way.

Is there a limit on scans?

Deducto Pro is designed for unlimited everyday use — scan as many receipts as your work generates. To keep the service fast and abuse-free for everyone, very high volumes are rate-limited: up to 100 scans per day and 400 in any rolling 30-day period. A typical freelancer scans 25–35 a month, and a tax-season catch-up still fits comfortably. If you legitimately need more, email us.

I forgot my password

If you created an account: on the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? — we'll email you a 6-digit code you can use to set a new password directly in the app.
